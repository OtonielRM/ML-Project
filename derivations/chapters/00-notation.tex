\chapter{Notation and Conventions}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Scalars $a, \lambda$; vectors $x \in \R^n$ (column vectors); matrices $A \in \R^{m \times n}$.
  \item Transpose $A\tp$ (macro \verb|\tp|); inner product $\inner{x}{y} = x\tp y$ (\verb|\inner{x}{y}|).
  \item Norms $\norm{x}_2$, $\norm{A}_F$ (\verb|\norm{...}|); absolute value $\abs{a}$ (\verb|\abs{...}|).
        Use \verb|\norm*{...}| for auto-sized delimiters.
  \item Column space $\colsp(A)$, null space $\nullsp(A)$, $\rank A$, $\tr A$, $\diag(d_1, \dots, d_n)$.
  \item Optimization: $\argmin_x f(x)$, $\argmax_x f(x)$.
\end{itemize}

\section*{How each entry is written}

Every result follows the same pattern: statement (\texttt{theorem}, \texttt{lemma}, \dots),
\verb|\source{...}| for where it comes from, the proof in my own words, then
\verb|\derived{YYYY-MM-DD}| once I can reproduce the full proof closed-book,
or \verb|\pending| until then.
